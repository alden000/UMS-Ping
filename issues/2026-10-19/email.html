2026-10-15 UK-made Kraken K3 Scout drone boat linked to strike on Russian vessel / Ukraine Port of Sochi, Russia (Black Sea) Industry sources say Kraken Technology's K3 Scout, a 28 ft (about 8.4 m) uncrewed attack boat, has been used in at least one live strike on a Russian vessel. Ambrey analysts describe 'double tap' attacks that pair aerial drones with surface drones often carrying up to half a tonne of explosives, and say the tempo has risen since July. More than 60 seafarers have died in the Black Sea since 2022, and Bulgaria and Romania are discussing Black Sea security with NATO. The Guardian

2026-10-13 Devil Ray T-24 USV / MARTAC (Maritime Tactical Systems) High-speed USV that can serve as a mothership for smaller USVs and third-party aerial and underwater drones, to be built in the US and in Taiwan. Taiwan's Coast Guard will acquire MARTAC USVs. The quantity, contract value and delivery schedule were not disclosed. Some hulls will be built in Edgewater, Florida and the rest in Taiwan by local partner Confucian, under a 2025 MoU with NCSIST. The craft is 8.4 m long with a 3 m beam, carries up to 816 kg, sprints above 50 kt and has a range of up to 600 nm in open water. Baird Maritime
2026-10-13 DANAE Armed USV Prototypes / SeaOwl, Orcas (SEAir-MBDA), Sirehna-Couach-Naval Group French Defence Innovation Agency (AID) programme for armed port-protection and escort USVs, with a human operator deciding on weapon use. The AID and the French Navy picked three teams from seven systems trialled near Toulon on 19–23 January. SeaOwl offers an optionally crewed boat with a non-lethal turret. The Orcas group, led by SEAir, offers a hydrofoil 'flying USV' that can be embarked on a frigate. Sirehna, Couach and Naval Group offer a 9 m modular USV. The teams have 12 months to refine their designs, and a final selection is expected by the end of 2027. Baird Maritime

Exail has started production of the Indonesian Navy's UMIS package, which includes four USVs plus a towed sonar and an influence sweep. 2026-10-12 Smart Acoustic Modems Natively Integrated on LAUV / Subnero & OceanScan MST Subnero's UnetStack acoustic network stack is linked to the LAUV's Dune software through a small open-source bridge that runs on the modem itself. The Acoustic Research Laboratory at NUS integrated Subnero modems natively into OceanScan MST's Light AUV. Neither the modem, the vehicle nor either side's software needed modification. The terminals operate on Viasat's global NextGen Ka-band network, including Mil-Ka. Each beam offers up to 500 MHz of instantaneous bandwidth, with up to 1 GHz aggregate in Ka-band, across HEO, GEO, MEO and emerging LEO constellations. Order quantity and value were not disclosed.
