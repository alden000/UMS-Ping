2026-10-15 GPS-free autonomous drone landing on moving ships / NAWCAD Vision-based guidance software that uses commercial cameras and motion sensors to recognise ship shapes and visual cues, and predicts deck motion in real time so a drone can land without GPS or a pilot. The US Navy's Naval Air Warfare Center Aircraft Division tested a tactical UAS that flew missions and landed on moving ships without GPS or pilot oversight.
